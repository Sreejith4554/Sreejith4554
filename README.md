## How I Approach Delivery

I focus on four practical questions:

1. **Is delivery visible?** — Clear milestones, owners, dependencies, risks and decisions.
2. **Are exceptions controlled?** — RAID discipline, escalation thresholds and accountable actions.
3. **Can coordination be simplified?** — Standardize processes and automate repetitive work where appropriate.
4. **Can management act on the information?** — Turn delivery data into concise, decision-ready reporting.
